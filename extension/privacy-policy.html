Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 16, 2026

Quick Summary: TalentScreen Autofill Extension stores all your data locally on your device. We never transmit, upload, or share your information with anyone. Your resume data never leaves your computer.

1. Overview

TalentScreen Autofill Extension ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome extension.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

The Extension stores the following information that you voluntarily upload:

  • Resume Data (JSON format): Personal information including name, email, phone, address, work history, education, skills, and professional summary
  • Resume Files: PDF or DOC files containing your resume
  • Custom Fields: EEO information (gender, ethnicity, veteran status, disability status), work authorization details, technical screening answers, and application preferences
  • Application Tracking: URLs of job application pages, form completion timestamps, and application status

2.2 Automatically Collected Data

  • Extension Usage: Form detection events and autofill success rates (stored locally for debugging purposes only)

3. How We Store Your Data

⚠️ IMPORTANT: All data is stored locally on your device using Chrome's local storage API.
  • ✅ Data never leaves your device
  • ✅ No cloud storage or external servers
  • ✅ No data transmission to third parties
  • ✅ No account creation required
  • ✅ You have complete control over your data

4. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Autofilling Job Applications: To automatically fill form fields on job application websites
  • Application Tracking: To help you track your job applications
  • Settings Management: To display and edit your resume information within the extension

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or transmit your data to any third parties.

Your data remains on your device at all times. The Extension does NOT:

  • Upload data to external servers
  • Share data with advertisers
  • Transmit data to analytics services
  • Send data to the extension developer
  • Use tracking pixels or cookies

6. Chrome Permissions Explained

6.1 storage

Purpose: Store your resume data and application tracking locally on your device
Data Access: Local device storage only

6.2 activeTab

Purpose: Detect job application forms on the page you're currently viewing
Data Access: Current tab content only when extension is active

6.3 tabs

Purpose: Automatically open the extension when you visit supported job sites
Data Access: URL of current tab (no page content)

6.4 sidePanel

Purpose: Display the extension interface
Data Access: None

6.5 contextMenus

Purpose: Provide right-click menu options for quick access
Data Access: None

6.6 Host Permissions (26+ ATS domains)

Purpose: Access job application forms on supported ATS platforms including Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, LinkedIn, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Taleo, and 20+ others
Data Access: Form fields on these specific domains only

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained in Chrome's local storage until you:

  • Manually delete it through the extension settings
  • Uninstall the extension
  • Clear Chrome's extension data

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your stored data through the extension settings
  • Modify: Edit your resume data anytime
  • Delete: Remove all data by uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage
  • Export: Download your data (JSON format available in settings)

9. Data Security

We implement security measures to protect your data:

  • All data stored locally using Chrome's secure storage API
  • No external data transmission
  • No backend servers or databases
  • No authentication tokens or credentials stored

10. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for use by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

The Extension does interact with job application websites (ATS platforms) to fill forms, but does not transmit your data to these sites beyond what you would manually enter.

12. International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there are no international data transfers.

13. Compliance

This Extension complies with:

  • Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

For EU Users (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and object to processing. Since all data is stored locally, you have full control through the extension settings.

For California Users (CCPA)

You have the right to know what personal information is collected, delete personal information, and opt-out of the sale of personal information. Note: We do not sell any data.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Displaying a notification in the extension (for significant changes)
